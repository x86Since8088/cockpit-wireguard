Clients

Monitoring is read-only and live. Two sections change state, both only with administrative access: host peers and listen ports via explicit, confirmed “wg set” commands, and the Clients section, which creates keys, writes peer entries, and can change forwarding and NAT rules. Generated client configurations contain a private key and are masked until you choose to reveal them.
